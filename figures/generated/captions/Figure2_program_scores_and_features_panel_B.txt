Figure 2. Exploratory NMF representation of patient-level abundance-weighted ligand–receptor expression-product scores. Panel A displays the ten saved NMF coefficients for 138 patients in the input-file order; it is not a patient-cluster analysis. Panel B displays the eight largest saved feature weights per program.
